# Code of Conduct

Deadlimit welcomes people who collaborate respectfully and in good faith.

Be constructive, stay focused on the technical work, respect different levels
of experience, and give clear feedback without harassment, threats, personal
attacks, discrimination, or publication of another person's private data.

The maintainer may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its
community. Context, severity, and repeated behavior will be considered.

For a sensitive conduct report, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/downlimit/Deadlimit/security/advisories/new)
and label the report as a code-of-conduct matter. Do not include third-party
licensed content or unnecessary personal data.
