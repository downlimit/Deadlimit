Shortcut bootstrap builds the Release Deadlimit Aggregator executable, refreshes Deadlimit Aggregator and Deadlimit Aggregator Updater icons, and points Deadlimit Aggregator.lnk directly at DeadlimitAggregator.exe.

The preferred local updater filename is DeadlimitAggregatorUpdater.bat. During migration the launcher also accepts the legacy untracked DEADLIMIT_LocalUpdater.bat and removes old Deadlimit/Updater shortcut names.
